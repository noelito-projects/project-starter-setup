Choose what to take from the starter

The starter is a set of helpers, checks, and know-how that Claude works with on a project. A project you already have can take all of it, or only the parts you choose from this page.

Each part says what it is for, what it gives you, and what it adds to your project. Some parts cannot work without others, so they bring those along. You can come back and add more later.

Two words come up often. A check looks over the work before it is saved, and a guard steps in before Claude takes a step it should not.

Always included

These come with every choice, because they keep private details out of what is saved and keep anything from reaching real people without your say, and the rest of the starter relies on them.
